# Backup & Recovery Strategy---ObjectivesProtect customer data.Minimize downtime.Enable rapid restoration.---Backup ScheduleContinuous WAL archiving.Nightly full backups.Weekly immutable snapshots.Monthly archive copies.---Recovery TargetsRecovery Point Objective (RPO)< 5 minutesRecovery Time Objective (RTO)< 60 minutes---TestingQuarterly recovery drills.Automated backup verification.Checksum validation.
