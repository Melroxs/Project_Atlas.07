# Core Business Rules1. Every Claim belongs to one Organization.2. Every Customer belongs to one Organization.3. Every Property belongs to one Customer.4. A Claim references one Property.5. A Property may have many Claims over time.6. Every Policy belongs to one Claim.7. Every Supplement belongs to one Claim.8. Every Document belongs to an Organization and optionally to a Claim.9. Every User belongs to one Organization.10. Claims cannot exist without an Organization.11. All timestamps are stored in UTC.12. Every mutation generates an Activity event.13. All foreign keys are enforced.14. Row-Level Security is mandatory for every tenant-owned table.15. Business records are soft deleted only.16. Version history is retained for auditability.17. AI-generated records always retain the originating model, prompt version, confidence score, and generation timestamp.
