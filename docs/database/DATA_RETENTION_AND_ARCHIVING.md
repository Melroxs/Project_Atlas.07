# Data Retention & Archiving---PurposeDefines lifecycle management for Atlas data.---Retention CategoriesOperational DataCustomer RecordsAudit RecordsAI LogsAnalyticsVoice SessionsExportsTemporary Files---Retention PrinciplesOperational records retained until customer deletion policy applies.Audit records retained according to compliance requirements.Temporary files automatically expired.Archived data remains searchable when permitted.---Legal HoldSupported.Overrides automatic deletion.---Business RulesRetention configurable by organization.Deletion policies auditable.
