# Entity Relationship ModelVersion: 1.0---# OverviewThe Atlas data model follows a hierarchical ownership structure.```Organization    │    ├── Users    ├── Teams    ├── Projects    ├── Claims    │      │    │      ├── Properties    │      ├── Customers    │      ├── Policies    │      ├── Insurance Companies    │      ├── Adjusters    │      ├── Supplements    │      ├── Documents    │      ├── Photos    │      ├── Evidence    │      ├── AI Decisions    │      └── Activity    │    └── Analytics```---# Primary Entities## OrganizationRoot tenant.Relationships:1:N Users1:N Claims1:N Projects1:N Documents1:N Teams---## UserBelongs to Organization.Relationships:N:1 OrganizationN:M Teams1:N Comments1:N Tasks1:N Voice Sessions---## ProjectContains multiple claims.---## ClaimCentral entity of Atlas.Relationships:1 Property1 Customer1 Insurance Policy1 Insurance Company1 AdjusterN SupplementsN DocumentsN PhotosN EvidenceN AI DecisionsN Activities---## PropertyLinked to one claim.Stores:AddressGPSBuilding TypeConstruction Details---## CustomerStores insured information.---## PolicyInsurance information.CoverageDeductibleCarrierLimits---## SupplementContains:VersionsLine ItemsEvidence LinksAI RecommendationsApprovals---## Line ItemBelongs to Supplement.May link to:EvidencePhotosCompliance RulesKnowledge Graph Nodes---## DocumentMay belong to:ClaimSupplementOrganizationAI Session---## PhotoBelongs to Claim.May link into Evidence Graph.---## EvidenceCore intelligence object.Connected to:PhotosDocumentsLine ItemsClaimsDecisionsComplianceKnowledge Graph---## AI DecisionStores:ReasoningConfidenceRecommendationRiskDecision Tree---## Atlas MemoryStores persistent AI memory.Scopes:GlobalOrganizationClaimUserConversation---# Cardinality RulesOrganization → Claims1:NClaim → Supplements1:NSupplement → Line Items1:NClaim → Documents1:NClaim → Photos1:NClaim → Evidence1:NEvidence → AI DecisionsN:MKnowledge Node → EvidenceN:MAI Session → Memory1:NVoice Session → Conversation1:N---# Ownership RulesEvery business entity belongs to exactly one organization.Every child record inherits ownership.No orphan records.Cascade deletion is never used for business records.Soft delete only.---# Referential IntegrityEvery FK enforced.Every relationship indexed.Every junction table composite keyed.Every timestamp UTC.UUIDs for all primary keys.
