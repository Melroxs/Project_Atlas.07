# AI Knowledge Rules1. Every embedding is associated with exactly one entity and one model version.2. Vector searches must respect organization boundaries through Row-Level Security.3. Every AI session records the model, version, prompt version, and execution metrics.4. Conversation history is immutable and linked to supporting documents where applicable.5. Voice transcripts are synchronized with conversation history and Atlas Memory.6. Every reasoning run stores all intermediate steps, confidence scores, and execution timings.7. Prompt templates are version-controlled and fully auditable.8. AI models are registered before use and retain historical capability metadata.9. Observability metrics are collected for every AI interaction.10. AI costs, latency, token usage, and error rates are available for analytics and optimization.11. All AI-generated artifacts inherit organization ownership and are protected by Row-Level Security.12. AI infrastructure must support reproducibility, explainability, and regulatory audit requirements.
