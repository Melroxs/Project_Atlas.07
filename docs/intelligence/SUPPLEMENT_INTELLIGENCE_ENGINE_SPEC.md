# SUPPLEMENT_INTELLIGENCE_ENGINE_SPEC.md## PurposeThe Atlas Supplement Intelligence Engine is the revenue optimization system for insurance restoration contractors. It analyzes claim data, photos, inspections, estimates, communications, historical outcomes, and organizational knowledge to identify legitimate, evidence-supported supplement opportunities that may otherwise be overlooked.Its goal is not to maximize supplement value at any cost—it is to maximize **accurate, compliant, and defensible revenue recovery**.---# VisionAtlas should answer questions such as:* What work is missing from this estimate?* Which supplement opportunities exist?* Why is this recommendation justified?* What evidence supports each recommendation?* What documentation is still required?* What is the estimated recoverable revenue?* How likely is approval?Every recommendation must be transparent, evidence-backed, and compliant.---# ObjectivesThe engine will:* Detect missing scope* Identify omitted line items* Recommend supplement opportunities* Estimate recoverable revenue* Generate supporting justification* Link recommendations to evidence* Assess approval likelihood* Improve over time from completed claims---# Guiding Principles* Evidence before recommendation* Compliance before revenue* Explainability over automation* Human approval before submission* Continuous learning from outcomes* Complete auditability---# InputsThe engine consumes information from:* Evidence Graph* Photo Intelligence* Document Intelligence* OCR extraction* Estimates* Xactimate exports* Inspection reports* Moisture documentation* Floor plans* Project timeline* Communications* Atlas Memory* Historical supplements* Carrier guidelines* Company SOPs---# OutputsThe engine produces:* Supplement recommendations* Missing scope analysis* Revenue estimate* Evidence package* Confidence score* Compliance assessment* Approval probability* Human review requirements---# Supplement Discovery PipelineStage 1Collect claim evidence↓Stage 2Analyze financial impact↓Stage 9Generate recommendation↓Stage 10Create evidence package---# Recommendation CategoriesStructuralWater Photos* Inspection findings* Moisture readings* Estimate comparisons* Building code references* Policy language (where available)* Customer communications* Adjuster communications* Historical approvals* Human observationsNo recommendation should exist without supporting evidence.---# Revenue EstimationFor every recommendation Atlas calculates:Estimated costEstimated recoverable valuePotential profit impactEstimated approval likelihoodExpected revenue contribution---# Approval ProbabilityApproval likelihood is calculated using factors such as:Evidence qualityEvidence completenessHistorical carrier approvalsHistorical adjuster behaviourCompliance readinessDocumentation qualityPhoto coverageInspection qualityEstimate consistency---# Approval CategoriesVery HighHighModerateLowUnknown---# Missing Scope DetectionAtlas should identify examples such as:Insulation omitted after drywall is the commercial intelligence core of the Atlas platform.
