# DECISION_CONFIDENCE_ENGINE_SPEC.md## PurposeThe Atlas Decision & Confidence Engine is the reasoning layer that transforms structured evidence into explainable recommendations. It evaluates the completeness, quality, consistency, and reliability of available information before recommending actions, assigning confidence scores, or escalating to a human reviewer.The engine does not make decisions based on a single document or image. Every recommendation must be supported by evidence collected from the Atlas Evidence Graph.---# VisionAtlas should be able to answer:* Should this supplement be submitted?* What evidence supports this recommendation?* What evidence is missing?* How confident is Atlas?* Why is confidence low?* Should a human review this claim?* What additional information would increase confidence?Every answer must be transparent and explainable.---# Design Principles* Evidence before conclusions* Explainability over opacity* Human oversight for uncertain decisions* Confidence is calculated, not guessed* Recommendations evolve as new evidence is added* Every recommendation is auditable* Every decision is reproducible---# ResponsibilitiesThe Decision Engine:* Evaluates available evidence* Calculates confidence scores* Detects contradictions* Identifies missing information* Assesses compliance readiness* Produces recommendations* Determines escalation requirements* Explains its reasoning---# InputsThe engine consumes information from:* Evidence Graph* Photo Intelligence* Document Intelligence* OCR results* Inspection reports* Estimates* Supplements* Communications* Timeline events* Atlas Memory* Compliance rules---# OutputsThe engine produces:* Recommendation* Confidence score* Evidence summary* Missing evidence list* Risk score* Compliance status* Escalation recommendation* Explanation trace---# Decision PipelineStage 1Collect evidence↓Stage 2Validate evidence↓Stage 3Detect inconsistencies↓Stage 4Assess completeness↓Stage 5Calculate confidence↓Stage 6Generate recommendation↓Stage 7Evaluate compliance↓Stage 8Escalate if required↓Stage 9Publish decision---# Recommendation TypesExamples:Submit supplementRequest additional evidenceRevise estimateSchedule inspectionObtain moisture readingsUpload supporting photosRequest documentationEscalate to estimatorEscalate to project managerEscalate to compliance reviewerNo action required---# Confidence CategoriesVery High95–100%High85–94%Moderate70–84%Low50–69%Very LowBelow 50%---# Confidence InputsConfidence is calculated using weighted factors:Evidence completenessPhoto qualityDocument qualityConsistencyHistorical similarityAI certaintyHuman verificationCompliance readinessTimeline consistencyData freshness---# Example WeightingEvidence completeness30%Evidence quality20%Cross-source consistency15%Historical success10%Human verification10%Compliance validation10%Model certainty5%Weights should remain configurable.---# Evidence CompletenessAtlas evaluates whether required evidence exists.Examples:Supporting photosInspection reportEstimateMeasurementsInvoicesMoisture readingsAdjuster communicationPolicy referencesMissing items reduce confidence.---# Consistency AnalysisThe engine detects contradictions.Examples:Estimate says drywall replaced.Photos show no drywall damage.Inspection reports mold.Estimate excludes mold remediation.Timeline indicates demolition occurred before inspection.Contradictions lower confidence and trigger review.---# Risk AssessmentRisk factors include:Insufficient documentationWeak photo evidenceConflicting reportsLarge supplement valuePolicy uncertaintyCompliance gapsHistorical denial patternsRepeated estimate revisions---# Risk CategoriesLowModerateHighCritical---# Compliance ReadinessEach recommendation receives one of:ReadyNeeds Supporting EvidenceNeeds Human ReviewBlockedCompliance checks include:Required documentationRequired inspectionsRequired photosRequired signaturesRequired policy references---# Escalation RulesAutomatically escalate when:Confidence below thresholdCritical evidence missingCompliance blockedContradictory information detectedLarge financial exposureRepeated AI uncertaintyManual override requested---# Human Review WorkflowHuman reviewer may:ApproveRejectModifyRequest more evidenceRecalculate confidenceOverride recommendationEvery action is recorded.---# ExplainabilityEach recommendation includes:SummaryEvidence usedEvidence ignoredConfidence calculationReasoning chainRisk factorsMissing evidenceSuggested next steps---# Reasoning Trace ExampleRecommendation:Submit supplementReason:Water damage detected in five photos.Inspection confirms moisture intrusion.Estimate excludes insulation replacement.Historical approvals support recommendation.Confidence:92%Missing Evidence:Customer acknowledgement---# Decision LifecycleDraft↓Pending Analysis↓Evidence Linked↓Recommendation Generated↓Human Review↓Approved↓Executed↓Archived---# Learning LoopFollowing claim completion, Atlas records:OutcomeApproval statusSettlement valueReview commentsTime to resolutionConfidence accuracyThese outcomes improve future recommendations.---# Confidence CalibrationThe engine continuously compares:Predicted confidenceActual outcomeDeviationCalibration adjustmentThis prevents systematic overconfidence.---# Decision ObjectsEach decision stores:Decision
