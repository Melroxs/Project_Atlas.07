# VOICE_ORCHESTRATION_ENGINE_SPEC.md## PurposeThe Atlas Voice Orchestration Engine is the conversational operating layer of the Atlas platform. It enables users to interact with Atlas using natural speech, allowing the AI to retrieve information, execute workflows, coordinate multiple intelligence engines, and explain decisions through a secure, context-aware voice interface.The Voice Orchestration Engine is not a standalone chatbot. It is an orchestration layer that coordinates the capabilities of the entire Atlas platform.---# VisionAtlas should feel like an AI operations partner.Users should be able to say:* "Atlas, review today's inspections."* "Atlas, open Claim 24-1038."* "Atlas, why are we recommending this supplement?"* "Atlas, show me the supporting evidence."* "Atlas, what work is still outstanding?"* "Atlas, draft an email to the adjuster."* "Atlas, summarize this project."Atlas should understand context, coordinate multiple AI systems, and provide clear, explainable responses.---# ObjectivesThe Voice Orchestration Engine will:* Understand spoken requests* Maintain conversational context* Coordinate platform services* Retrieve relevant organizational knowledge* Execute approved workflows* Explain AI reasoning* Support hands-free field operations* Improve productivity for office and field teams---# Design Principles* Voice-first, not voice-only* Explainable responses* Secure authentication* Context-aware conversations* Human confirmation for sensitive actions* Fast response times* Accessible across devices---# High-Level ArchitectureUser Speech↓Speech Recognition↓Intent Detection↓Context Assembly↓Workflow Orchestrator↓AI Intelligence Engines↓Response Generation↓Speech Synthesis↓User---# Core options:Authenticated user sessionOptional voice verificationMulti-factor authentication for high-risk actionsSession timeoutRole-based permissions---# PrivacyVoice recordings should support configurable retention policies.Organizations may choose to:Store audioStore transcripts onlyDelete recordings after processingRetention must comply with applicable legal and contractual requirements.---# Error HandlingIf Atlas is uncertain it should:Request clarificationOffer likely interpretationsDisplay supporting informationAvoid executing ambiguous commandsNever invent missing information.---# API EndpointsPOST /voice/transcribePOST /voice/intentPOST /voice/respondPOST /voice/executePOST /voice/synthesizeGET
