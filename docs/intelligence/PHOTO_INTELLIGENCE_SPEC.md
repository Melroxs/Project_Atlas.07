# PHOTO_INTELLIGENCE_SPEC.md## OverviewThe Photo Intelligence Engine is Atlas's multimodal computer vision system. Its purpose is to transform construction and restoration photos into structured, searchable evidence that supports insurance claims, supplements, compliance validation, and organizational knowledge.Rather than treating photos as simple attachments, Atlas interprets every image as evidence.---# ObjectivesThe engine must:* Analyze restoration photographs automatically.* Detect visible damage.* Identify building materials and components.* Estimate affected areas.* Compare before-and-after progress.* Extract metadata and contextual information.* Generate structured evidence.* Link findings directly to claims and supplements.* Provide confidence scores for every AI-generated observation.---# InputsSupported sources include:* Mobile application uploads* Tablet uploads* Desktop uploads* Drone imagery* Thermal images (future)* Moisture camera images (future)* 360° inspections (future)* Customer-submitted photos* Adjuster photos---# Supported Formats* JPG* JPEG* PNG* HEIC* WEBP* TIFF (future)---# Image Processing Pipeline1. Upload image2. Validate image quality3. Extract metadata4. Remove duplicates5. Generate thumbnail6. Perform AI image analysis7. Detect damage8. Identify materials9. Estimate affected regions10. Generate structured observations11. Create evidence objects12. Link to Evidence Graph13. Store embeddings for semantic search---# Metadata ExtractionAtlas should automatically capture:* GPS coordinates* Capture time* Camera orientation* Device information* Resolution* File size* Claim ID* Project ID* Room* Inspector* Upload source---# Damage DetectionInitial MVP should detect:* Water damage* Mold* Fire damage* Smoke staining* Wind damage* Hail damage* Roof damage* Ceiling damage* Drywall damage* Flooring damage* Cabinet damage* Window damage* Structural damageEach detected item includes:* Bounding region* Damage category* Severity* Confidence score* Suggested repair action---# Material RecognitionIdentify:* Drywall* Insulation* Roofing shingles* Metal roofing* Wood framing* Flooring* Cabinets* Countertops* Tile* Carpet* Hardwood* Laminate* Windows* Doors* Paint* Trim---# Room ClassificationAutomatically classify images into:* Kitchen* Bathroom* Bedroom* Living room* Dining room* Laundry* Garage* Attic* Basement* Exterior* Roof* Crawl space---# Before/After ComparisonAtlas should:* Match photos by room* Detect repairs completed* Highlight remaining damage* Produce change summaries* Generate comparison reports---# Evidence ExtractionEvery image becomes structured evidence.Example:Photo↓Water stain↓Ceiling drywall↓Living room↓Moisture likely↓Evidence object↓Linked to supplement recommendation---# Confidence ScoringEach observation includes:* Detection confidence* Material confidence* Room confidence* Damage confidence* Overall confidenceConfidence ranges:* High* Medium* Low---# Integration with Evidence GraphEach image links to:* Claim* Project* Estimate* Line item* Supplement* Inspection* Document* Communication* Timeline event---# Semantic SearchUsers can search naturally:* "Show ceiling water damage."* "Find missing insulation."* "Kitchen mold."* "Roof hail damage."* "Photos supporting drywall replacement."---# OutputsThe engine produces:* Structured observations* Damage reports* Material inventory* Room classification* Evidence objects* AI summaries* Confidence scores* Search embeddings---# API EndpointsPOST /photos/uploadPOST /photos/analyzeGET /photos/{id}GET /photos/{id}/analysisGET /photos/searchPOST /photos/compare---# Database ObjectsPhotoPhotoAnalysisDamageDetectionMaterialDetectionEvidenceReferencePhotoEmbeddingPhotoRelationship---# Performance GoalsPhoto upload:< 3 secondsInitial analysis:< 10 secondsSemantic search:< 2 secondsEvidence generation:< 5 seconds---# MVP ScopeIncluded:* Photo upload* AI analysis* Damage detection* Material recognition* Room classification* Evidence generation* Confidence scoring* Evidence Graph integrationDeferred:* Thermal imagery* Drone imagery* Moisture sensors* Live video analysis* 3D reconstruction* Automated measurements* Real-time field guidance---# Success Metrics* Detection accuracy* Classification accuracy* Search relevance* Evidence completeness* User acceptance rate* Reduction in manual review time* Increase in supported supplement value---# Future Enhancements* Live camera guidance* Automatic room measurement* Moisture estimation* AI repair recommendations* Cost estimation* Progress tracking* Insurance policy validation* Damage timeline reconstruction* Digital twin generation---# VisionThe Photo Intelligence Engine transforms every uploaded image into actionable, explainable evidence. By combining computer vision with Atlas's Evidence Graph and Supplement Intelligence Engine, photos become searchable knowledge that improves claim quality, strengthens supplement recommendations, and accelerates insurance restoration workflows.
