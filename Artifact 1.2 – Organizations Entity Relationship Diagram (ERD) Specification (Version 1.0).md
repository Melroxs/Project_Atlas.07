Artifact 1.2 – Organizations Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) Specification (Version 1.0)PurposeThe organizations table is the root entity in Atlas.Atlas is a multi-tenant SaaS platform, meaning every piece of business data belongs to exactly one organization. Tenant isolation starts here.Organization Relationship Map                                    ORGANIZATIONS                                          │
