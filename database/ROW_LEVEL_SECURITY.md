# Row-Level Security (RLS)---PurposeEnforces tenant isolation and fine-grained authorization.---PoliciesOrganization IsolationUsers may access only rows belonging to their organization.---Role-Based AccessPermissions evaluated before data access.---Ownership RulesRecords inherit organization ownership.---AI ServicesBackground AI processes operate through service roles with scoped access.---Business RulesRLS enabled on every tenant-owned table.No bypass outside trusted service roles.Policies tested automatically.
