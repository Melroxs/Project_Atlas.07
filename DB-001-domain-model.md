# DB-001 – Atlas Domain Model**Document ID:** DB-001**Version:** 1.0**Status:** Approved**Owner:** Atlas Engineering---# PurposeThis document defines the business domains that make up the Atlas platform.It is the authoritative source for:- Database entities- Domain ownership- Business relationships- Aggregate boundaries- Service ownership- API boundariesNo database table should exist unless it belongs to one of the domains defined here.---# Domain PhilosophyAtlas is designed using Domain-Driven Design (DDD).Each domain represents a distinct business capability.Domains should be:- Independently understandable- Loosely coupled- Highly cohesive- Clearly owned- Scalable---# Atlas Domain Map```Atlas Platform├── Platform│├── Identity & Access│├── CRM│├── Claims│├── Documents│├── Photos│├── Evidence Intelligence│├── Artificial Intelligence│├── Supplements│├── Compliance│├── Workflow│├── Communications│├── Reporting│├── Voice Intelligence│├── Integrations│├── Administration│└── Infrastructure```---# Domain Definitions## PlatformResponsible for:- Organizations- Licensing- Tenant management- Feature flags- Global settingsPrimary Entities:- Organization- OrganizationSettings- Subscription- FeatureFlag---## Identity & AccessResponsible for:- Authentication- Authorization- Users- Teams- Roles- PermissionsPrimary Entities:- User- Team- Role- Permission- Session- APIKey---## CRMResponsible for customer information.Primary Entities:- Customer- Contact- Property- Address---## ClaimsResponsible for insurance claims.Primary Entities:- Claim- Policy- Adjuster- InsuranceCompany- ClaimStatus- TimelineEvent---## DocumentsResponsible for document storage and processing.Primary Entities:- Document- DocumentVersion- OCRResult- DocumentCategory---## PhotosResponsible for visual evidence.Primary Entities:- Photo- Album- Annotation- DamageMarker- PhotoMetadata---## Evidence IntelligenceResponsible for connecting evidence.Primary Entities:- EvidenceNode- EvidenceLink- EvidenceRelationship- EvidenceScore---## Artificial IntelligenceResponsible for reasoning.Primary Entities:- AISession- Prompt- AIConversation- AIRecommendation- ConfidenceScore---## SupplementsResponsible for revenue recovery.Primary Entities:- Supplement- SupplementItem- Estimate- EstimateLineItem- Submission---## ComplianceResponsible for validation.Primary Entities:- Rule- Validation- ComplianceReview- ComplianceViolation---## WorkflowResponsible for operational automation.Primary Entities:- Workflow- WorkflowStep- Task- Assignment- AutomationRule---## CommunicationsResponsible for communication.Primary Entities:- Notification- Email- SMS- PushNotification- ActivityFeed---## ReportingResponsible for analytics.Primary Entities:- Dashboard- Report- Metric- KPI- ExecutiveSummary---## Voice IntelligenceResponsible for conversational interaction.Primary Entities:- VoiceSession- VoiceCommand- VoiceTranscript- VoiceContext- VoiceAction---## IntegrationsResponsible for external systems.Primary Entities:- Integration- Webhook- APIConnection- SyncJob- Credential---## AdministrationResponsible for configuration.Primary Entities:- AuditLog- OrganizationSetting- SystemConfiguration- UserPreference---## InfrastructureResponsible for technical operations.Primary Entities:- BackgroundJob- Event- QueueMessage- CacheEntry- DeploymentLog---# Aggregate RootsThe primary aggregate roots are:- Organization- User- Customer- Property- Claim- Document- Photo- Supplement- Workflow- AI SessionThese entities own the lifecycle of their related records.---# Cross
