# COMPLIANCE-001 — Atlas Compliance Engine Architecture**Document ID:** COMPLIANCE-001**Version:** 1.0**Status:** Draft**Owner:** Atlas Engineering---# PurposeThis document defines the architecture of the Atlas Compliance Engine.The Compliance Engine validates that Atlas recommendations are:- Supported by evidence.- Consistent with workflow requirements.- Properly documented.- Review-ready.---# Business ProblemRestoration companies face challenges:- Missing documentation.- Incomplete claim files.- Unsupported scope additions.- Poor evidence organization.- Compliance risk during reviews.Atlas reduces these issues by continuously checking claim readiness.---# Compliance Engine PrincipleAtlas does not replace legal or regulatory professionals.Atlas provides:
